# Chapter 10, Verse 9

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
मच्चित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम्।

कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च।।10.9।।

**English Translation:**
With their minds fixed on Me and their lives devoted to Me, the wise enlighten one another and constantly speak about Me. In doing so, they experience satisfaction and deep joy.

**Meaning & Purport:**
Krishna now describes the inner and social life of those who truly understand the principle explained in the previous verse. When a person recognizes a deeper source and purpose behind existence, their attention naturally begins to revolve around that understanding. The phrase "maccitta" indicates a mind that repeatedly returns to a higher principle rather than being constantly pulled by distractions.

For Arjuna, this description presents a contrast to the mental turmoil he experienced earlier. His thoughts were scattered between fear, attachment, and imagined consequences. Krishna shows that when the mind is anchored in a stable understanding of reality, conversation, reflection, and relationships begin to revolve around meaningful ideas rather than anxiety or conflict.

An important detail in the verse is that such individuals "enlighten one another." Wisdom is not developed in isolation. People who pursue understanding naturally share insights, question each other, and deepen their clarity through discussion. This collective reflection strengthens conviction and prevents the mind from drifting back into confusion.

The result is a form of satisfaction and joy that does not depend on external achievements. When the mind is engaged with meaningful understanding and shared exploration of truth, a quieter but more stable form of fulfillment appears. This kind of satisfaction grows from clarity and purpose rather than from temporary pleasures or social validation.

**Modern Applications:**
meaningful conversations, intellectual community, shared learning, purpose driven thinking, deep fulfillment, focus on meaningful ideas
