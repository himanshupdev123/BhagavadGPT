# Chapter 10, Verse 3

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
यो मामजमनादिं च वेत्ति लोकमहेश्वरम्।

असम्मूढः स मर्त्येषु सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते।।10.3।।

**English Translation:**
One who knows Me as unborn, without beginning, and as the supreme Lord of all worlds, such a person among mortals is free from delusion and is released from all sinful reactions.

**Meaning & Purport:**
Krishna now explains the practical consequence of the idea introduced in the previous verse. If the ultimate reality is the source of everything, then understanding that reality correctly transforms a person's entire perception of life. Krishna describes three aspects of this understanding: the divine is unborn, without beginning, and the supreme governing principle behind the universe.

To recognize this is to become "asammudha," meaning free from confusion. Human beings often live in a state of psychological confusion because they treat temporary circumstances as absolute reality. Success, failure, praise, and loss appear overwhelmingly important when they are seen as the center of existence. This misunderstanding produces fear, anxiety, and moral compromise.

Arjuna's crisis on the battlefield came from this same confusion. He was overwhelmed by the immediate emotional consequences of the war and temporarily lost sight of the larger order of duty and purpose. Krishna explains that when a person understands the deeper foundation of existence, the mind becomes steadier. Life's events are no longer seen as random chaos but as part of a larger order.

Freedom from "sin" in this verse refers to freedom from the destructive patterns that arise from confusion such as selfish action, fear driven decisions, and attachment to temporary outcomes. When understanding becomes clear, actions become more aligned with truth and responsibility, and the mind gradually becomes free from the consequences of misguided choices.

**Modern Applications:**
overcoming confusion, clarity of worldview, freedom from destructive habits, seeing the bigger picture, philosophical understanding of life, mental clarity
