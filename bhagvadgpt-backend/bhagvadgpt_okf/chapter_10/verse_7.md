# Chapter 10, Verse 7

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः।

सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः।।10.7।।

**English Translation:**
One who truly understands My divine manifestations and the power by which I sustain the universe becomes firmly established in spiritual union with Me. Of this there is no doubt.

**Meaning & Purport:**
Krishna now explains the purpose behind the teachings he is about to give in this chapter. He will describe his "vibhutis," meaning the various ways divine intelligence and power are expressed throughout the universe. These manifestations are not meant to impress Arjuna with cosmic grandeur. Their purpose is to help him recognize the underlying unity behind the complexity of the world.

Earlier in the dialogue, Arjuna's mind was shaken by emotional conflict. His thoughts moved between attachment, fear, and moral uncertainty. Such instability arises when a person sees life as a series of disconnected events and pressures. Krishna now explains that when someone understands the deeper principle behind existence, the mind gains stability.

The verse uses the phrase "avikampena yoga," meaning a form of inner connection that cannot be shaken. Psychological steadiness develops when a person recognizes that there is an underlying order behind life rather than random chaos. This understanding gradually reduces doubt and emotional turbulence.

For Arjuna, learning about the divine presence within the world will strengthen his conviction and remove hesitation. For modern individuals, the same principle applies at a psychological level. When people understand the deeper principles guiding their life such as purpose, responsibility, and alignment with truth, their decisions become steadier. Doubt loses its power because their actions are grounded in a stable framework of understanding.

**Modern Applications:**
mental stability, overcoming doubt, seeing the bigger picture, purpose driven thinking, inner conviction, clarity of worldview
