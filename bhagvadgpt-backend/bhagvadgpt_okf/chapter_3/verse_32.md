# Chapter 3, Verse 32

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
ये त्वेतदभ्यसूयन्तो नानुतिष्ठन्ति मे मतम्।

सर्वज्ञानविमूढांस्तान्विद्धि नष्टानचेतसः।।3.32।।

**English Translation:**
But those who, out of envy or disregard, do not follow these teachings of Mine and do not practice them properly—know them to be completely deluded in all knowledge and lost in understanding.

**Meaning & Purport:**
In this verse, Krishna draws attention to a psychological barrier that prevents people from benefiting from wisdom: resistance driven by ego and envy. The teachings of karma yoga presented earlier are meant to guide a person toward disciplined action without attachment. However, not everyone accepts such guidance. Some reject it not because it lacks truth, but because their ego resists being corrected.

Just as Arjuna initially struggled to accept Krishna’s instruction due to emotional confusion and attachment, many people today resist advice that challenges their habits or beliefs. When the mind is dominated by pride or defensiveness, it stops learning. A person may possess education, intelligence, or information, but without openness and humility, that knowledge becomes ineffective. This is why Krishna describes such individuals as "deluded in all knowledge." Their problem is not lack of information, but a closed attitude toward truth.

The verse highlights a universal principle: wisdom only transforms a person when it is sincerely practiced. Merely hearing good advice, reading philosophy, or discussing spiritual ideas does not create change. Transformation begins when a person is willing to question their ego and apply the teachings in daily action. Without this willingness, even the clearest guidance becomes useless.
