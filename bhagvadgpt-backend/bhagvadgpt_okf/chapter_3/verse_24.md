# Chapter 3, Verse 24

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
उत्सीदेयुरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम्।
सङ्करस्य च कर्ता स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः।।3.24।।

**English Translation:**
If I should cease to perform My duties, all these worlds would fall into ruin. I would be the cause of chaos, and I would destroy all these beings.

**Meaning & Purport:**
This verse highlights the catastrophic consequences of avoiding responsibility. Krishna warns that if He were to abandon His duties, the entire social and cosmic order would collapse into chaos, leading to the destruction of peace for all beings. In doing so, He completely reverses Arjuna's flawed logic.

Just as Arjuna felt paralyzed by his attachments and duties, arguing that fighting the war would cause societal breakdown and corrupt future generations, Krishna points out the exact opposite: it is the abandonment of righteous duty by capable individuals that truly destroys a society. When the strong and capable retreat into passive escapism, they create a vacuum that is quickly filled by confusion and moral decay.

Modern individuals frequently suffer from this same delusion. We often convince ourselves that walking away from a difficult relationship, a challenging career, or a civic duty is the 'peaceful' choice. However, when competent people neglect their responsibilities out of fear or a desire for comfort, the systems they support begin to crumble. A parent who checks out emotionally, or a manager who avoids difficult decisions, inadvertently breeds anxiety, instability, and chaos within their sphere of influence. True peace is never achieved by abandoning the post; it is sustained by the courageous execution of one's duty, which holds the fabric of the community together.

**Modern Applications:**
responsibility, consequences of inaction, leadership, avoidance, chaos, social order
