# Chapter 3, Verse 26

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम्।
जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान् युक्तः समाचरन्।।3.26।।

**English Translation:**
The wise should not create conflict in the minds of the ignorant who are attached to the results of their actions. Rather, by performing their own duties with discipline and devotion, the wise should inspire others to engage in all activities.

**Meaning & Purport:**
This verse addresses a critical psychological principle: meeting people where they are. Krishna advises that a person with deep understanding should not forcefully dismantle the belief systems of those who are still driven by material rewards. Disrupting someone’s worldview before they are emotionally or intellectually ready only leads to confusion, loss of faith, and eventually, total inaction.

Just as Arjuna felt paralyzed by his attachments and duties, struggling to reconcile his high-level principles with the messy reality of war, many people today feel overwhelmed when presented with philosophies that demand immediate, total detachment. If a leader or mentor simply tells an ambitious person that 'results don't matter,' it may cause that person to lose their drive entirely because they don't yet have a higher purpose to replace their ego-driven motivation.

Instead of lecturing or criticizing, Krishna suggests that the wise should lead through 'inclusive inspiration.' By performing your own work with excellence and a calm, balanced mind, you provide a living example of a better way to live. You don't tell them their motivation is wrong; you show them a higher quality of action. This allows others to grow at their own pace, moving naturally from being driven by anxiety and greed to being driven by duty and contribution. True change is never forced from the outside; it is inspired by witnessing the peace and effectiveness of those who have already mastered themselves.

**Modern Applications:**
leadership, emotional intelligence, mentorship, meeting people where they are, influence, work ethic
