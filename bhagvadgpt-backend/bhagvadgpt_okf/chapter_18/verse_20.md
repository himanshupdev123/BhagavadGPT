# Chapter 18, Verse 20

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
सर्वभूतेषु येनैकं भावमव्ययमीक्षते।
अविभक्तं विभक्तेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि सात्त्विकम्।।18.20।।

**English Translation:**
That knowledge by which one undivided spiritual nature is seen in all living entities, though they are divided into innumerable forms, you should understand to be in the mode of goodness (Sattvic).

**Meaning & Purport:**
Krishna begins his diagnostic of human perspective with the highest tier: **Sattvic Knowledge**. In 2026, we often confuse 'knowledge' with 'data accumulation' or 'technical skill.' But Krishna defines knowledge as a **Lens of Perception**. Sattvic knowledge is the ability to see the unified whole (*avibhaktam*) amidst the fragmented parts (*vibhakteṣu*).

In our hyper-siloed, algorithmically divided world, this is the ultimate cognitive superpower: **Holistic Systems Thinking paired with Radical Empathy.**

Krishna breaks down this lens:
1. **Sarva-bhūteṣu (In all beings):** This is the end of "Us vs. Them." When you look at a competitor, a difficult family member, or someone with opposing political views, you don't just see the external friction. You see the shared human operating system—the same fears, the same desire for safety, and the same underlying consciousness.
2. **Ekaṁ bhāvam avyayam (One imperishable nature):** You recognize that despite the different "avatars" we wear (different jobs, bodies, cultures), the core source code is identical. 



Psychologically, Sattvic knowledge cures **Zero-Sum Thinking**. When you operate from a fragmented perspective, every interaction is a battle: if they win, I lose. But when you possess Sattvic vision, you see the ecosystem. A CEO with Sattvic knowledge doesn't just look at their profit margins; they see how their product impacts the environment, their employees' mental health, and the end consumer. They optimize for the whole board, not just their own piece.

This isn't just spiritual poetry; it is the prerequisite for **High-Level Leadership**. You cannot lead a team, a family, or a community if you only see their divisions. You have to be able to spot the invisible threads connecting them.

Think about a person or a group you currently feel intensely divided from or in competition with. Would you like to try a 'Sattvic Lens' exercise to find the *avibhaktam* (the undivided/shared reality) between you and them, just to lower your own internal resistance?
