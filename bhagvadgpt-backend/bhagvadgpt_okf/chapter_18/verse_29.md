# Chapter 18, Verse 29

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
बुद्धेर्भेदं धृतेश्चैव गुणतस्त्रिविधं श्रृणु।
प्रोच्यमानमशेषेण पृथक्त्वेन धनञ्जय।।18.29।।

**English Translation:**
O winner of wealth, now please listen as I tell you in detail of the three kinds of understanding and determination according to the three modes of material nature.

**Meaning & Purport:**
Krishna has already audited our Perspective (Knowledge), our Actions, and our Identity (The Doer). Now, he moves into the 'Command Center' of the human psyche. He introduces the final two components that determine whether we actually execute our goals or just daydream about them: **Buddhi** (Intelligence/Judgment) and **Dhṛti** (Determination/Willpower).

In 2026 terminology, Krishna is shifting the conversation to **Executive Function** and **Cognitive Endurance**.

1.  **Buddhi (Understanding/Discernment):** This is your internal GPS. It’s the ability to distinguish between what is actually a high-leverage move and what is just busywork; what is a healthy boundary and what is an excuse. 
2.  **Dhṛti (Determination/Grit):** This is your battery pack. It’s the mental fortitude required to hold your focus when the initial dopamine hit of a 'new project' wears off and the 'boring middle' begins.



Krishna addresses Arjuna as *Dhanañjaya* (Winner of Wealth). This is a strategic psychological nudge. He is reminding Arjuna that true 'wealth'—whether financial, emotional, or spiritual—is not won by luck, but by the precise calibration of these two internal engines. 

If your **Buddhi** is off, you will run very fast in the wrong direction (working hard at a job that drains you). If your **Dhṛti** is off, you will have great ideas but never finish anything. Krishna is about to show us how to 'debug' these two systems across the three modes (Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas).

Think about your current biggest goal. Do you feel you lack the **Direction** (not sure what the right move is) or the **Drive** (you know what to do but can't stay consistent)? Would you like to proceed to **Verse 30** to see what 'Sattvic Intelligence' looks like?
