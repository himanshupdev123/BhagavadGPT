# Chapter 18, Verse 76

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
राजन्संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य संवादमिममद्भुतम्।
केशवार्जुनयोः पुण्यं हृष्यामि च मुहुर्मुहुः।।18.76।।

**English Translation:**
O King, as I repeatedly recall this wondrous and holy dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna, I am thrilled with joy at every moment.

**Meaning & Purport:**
Sanjaya’s reaction here illustrates the psychological power of contemplation (Manana). He emphasizes that he is remembering the conversation 'again and again.' This is not a redundant repetition of facts, but a process of cognitive integration. When a person encounters a truth that fundamentally shifts their perspective, the mind naturally returns to it to solidify the new mental framework. 

Just as Arjuna was the direct recipient of the cure for his paralysis, Sanjaya, as the witness, shows that the benefit of profound wisdom is available to anyone who observes it with focus. For a modern individual, this explains why we feel a 'second wave' of inspiration when we revisit a powerful idea or conversation. The initial experience provides the seed, but the repeated reflection allows the wisdom to take root in the subconscious. 

Sanjaya describes his state as 'hrisyami'—a thrill of joy. This is the psychological 'Aha!' moment of clarity becoming a permanent emotional state. It suggests that once the ego's confusion is removed, the natural state of the mind is one of appreciative joy. By repeatedly recalling these higher truths, we create a positive feedback loop that protects us from the draining noise of daily stress and cynicism. Reflection turns information into a lived experience.

**Modern Applications:**
reflection, positive reinforcement, lifelong learning, mental integration, joy of discovery, perspective
