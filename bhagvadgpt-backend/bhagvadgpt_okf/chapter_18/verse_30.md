# Chapter 18, Verse 30

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च कार्याकार्ये भयाभये।
बन्धं मोक्षं च या वेत्ति बुद्धिः सा पार्थ सात्त्विकी।।18.30।।

**English Translation:**
O son of Pritha, that understanding by which one knows what ought to be done and what ought not to be done, what is to be feared and what is not to be feared, what is binding and what is liberating, is in the mode of goodness (Sattvic).

**Meaning & Purport:**
Krishna defines Sattvic Intelligence not as 'IQ,' but as High-Resolution Discernment. In 2026, where we are bombarded by choice overload and 'infinite scroll' lifestyles, this version of intelligence is the ultimate filter for your sanity.

He breaks it down into four critical binary pairs that a healthy mind must navigate:

1. Pravṛttiṁ vs. Nivṛttiṁ (When to Lean In vs. When to Pull Back): This is the intelligence of Rhythm. It’s knowing when to launch a project and when to enter 'monk mode.' It’s the difference between productive effort and mindless busywork.
2. Kārya vs. Akārya (Duty vs. Distraction): This is the intelligence of Integrity. It’s knowing which tasks actually move the needle for your life and which are just 'shadow work' designed to make you feel busy while avoiding the real challenge.
3. Bhaya vs. Abhaya (Rational Fear vs. Irrational Anxiety): This is the intelligence of Emotional Regulation. It’s the ability to distinguish between a healthy fear of real risk and the paralyzing, ego-driven anxiety of 'what will people think?'
4. Bandha vs. Mokṣa (Bondage vs. Freedom): This is the intelligence of Consequence. It’s realizing that some 'shortcuts' or dopamine hits (like doom-scrolling or toxic validation) actually trap you in a cycle of dependency, while hard discipline actually sets you free.



Psychologically, Sattvic intelligence is like having a clear, well-lit dashboard for your life. You aren't guessing; you see the 'road' exactly as it is. Most of our modern stress comes from 'Grey Area Thinking'—not being sure if we should say yes or no, stay or go. A Sattvic mind cuts through the grey with surgical precision.

Think about a decision you’ve been 'looping' on lately. If you apply the filter of 'Freedom vs. Bondage,' does the choice become clearer? Would you like to see how Rajasic Intelligence (Verse 31) gets these binaries confused and creates the 'Always-Anxious' mindset?
