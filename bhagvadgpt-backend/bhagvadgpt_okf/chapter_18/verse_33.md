# Chapter 18, Verse 33

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
धृत्या यया धारयते मनःप्राणेन्द्रियक्रियाः।
योगेनाव्यभिचारिण्या धृतिः सा पार्थ सात्त्विकी।।18.33।।

**English Translation:**
O son of Pritha, that determination which is unswerving, which is sustained with steadfastness by yoga practice, and which thus controls the activities of the mind, life and senses, is in the mode of goodness (Sattvic).

**Meaning & Purport:**
Krishna now moves from *how we think* to *how we hold on*. He introduces **Sattvic Dhṛti**, which we can think of as **High-Resolution Willpower**. In 2026, we are obsessed with 'grit,' but we usually treat it like a finite battery that we drain until we crash. Krishna describes a different, more sustainable model of endurance.

Sattvic willpower is the ability to maintain a 'Steady State' by regulating three specific internal systems:

1.  **Manas (The Mind):** Regulating the internal monologue. It’s the willpower to stop a negative thought spiral before it consumes your afternoon.
2.  **Prāṇa (The Life-Force/Nervous System):** Regulating your energy. This is the ability to stay calm under pressure, managing your breath and heart rate so you don't flip into 'fight or flight' mode during a difficult meeting.
3.  **Indriya (The Senses):** Regulating external inputs. This is the discipline to ignore the dopamine hit of a notification or a distraction when you are doing deep work.

 

Krishna says this willpower is *avyabhicāriṇyā* (unswerving). It’s not a manic burst of energy; it’s a quiet, unbreakable consistency. Psychologically, this is **Executive Control** at its peak. You aren't forcing yourself through sheer 'white-knuckle' effort; you are simply holding your systems in alignment through *Yoga* (integration).

While Rajasic grit is fueled by caffeine and anxiety, and Tamasic grit is just stubbornness, Sattvic grit is fueled by **Clarity**. You stay determined because you clearly see the value of the path, and you have the internal tools to manage the friction of the journey.

Think about a habit you’re trying to build. Are you trying to 'force' it with raw effort, or are you working on regulating your mind, energy, and senses to make the habit sustainable? Would you like to explore **Verse 34**, which describes **Rajasic Determination**—the kind that makes us work hard for the wrong reasons?
