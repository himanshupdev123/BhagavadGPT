# Chapter 18, Verse 26

**Sanskrit (Devanagari):**
मुक्तसङ्गोऽनहंवादी धृत्युत्साहसमन्वितः।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योर्निर्विकारः कर्ता सात्त्विक उच्यते।।18.26।।

**English Translation:**
The worker who is free from all material attachments and false ego, who is endowed with great enthusiasm and determination, and who is unperturbed in success or failure, is said to be a worker in the mode of goodness (Sattvic).

**Meaning & Purport:**
Having diagnosed types of Knowledge and Action, Krishna now defines the third pillar of performance: the **Kartā** (The Doer/Operator). In 2026, we might call this the profile of a **Level 5 Leader** or a **Flow-State Professional**. 

Krishna outlines five psychological traits that make an individual bulletproof to the volatility of the modern world:

1.  **Mukta-saṅgaḥ (Detached Operator):** They are deeply involved in the work but not 'fused' with it. They don't need the work to provide them with a sense of identity or worth; they bring their worth *to* the work. 
2.  **Anahaṁ-vādī (Speech without Ego):** They don't waste energy on self-promotion, credit-seeking, or saying "I did this." They focus on what needs to be done, not who gets the applause. 
3.  **Dhṛti (Endurance/Fortitude):** When things get boring or difficult, they have the grit to stay the course. This is the long-term consistency that beats short-term intensity.
4.  **Utsāha (Enthusiasm):** This is the game-changer. Despite being detached, they are not apathetic. They are incredibly energized. This is **Intrinsic Motivation**—the joy is in the doing, not just the winning.
5.  **Nirvikāraḥ (Equanimity in Outcomes):** Whether the KPI is met (*siddhi*) or the project flops (*asiddhi*), their internal emotional state remains stable. They don't go through manic highs or depressive lows based on external metrics.



Psychologically, this is the ultimate antidote to 'Quiet Quitting.' A Sattvic worker is the opposite of a quiet quitter; they are loud in their effort but quiet in their ego. They represent a sustainable model of high performance because their energy isn't being drained by the 'friction' of pride, fear, or the need for constant validation. 

Think about your current professional or personal 'avatar.' Which of these five traits feels the most natural to you, and which one feels like your biggest 'growth area' right now? Would you like to explore a specific mental protocol to increase your **Utsāha** (enthusiasm) while maintaining your **Mukta-saṅgaḥ** (detachment) for a project you're currently working on?
